Your game discs

Holiday Island needs its original CD. Add an image of your disc — .iso, .bin/.cue or .mdf/.mds — and choose English or German depending on which disc you add. Nothing is uploaded; the files stay on this device.

Mods

Without mods the game looks and plays exactly like the 1996 original. Mods add filters, a bigger island view, comfort features and fixes. Changes apply the next time you start the game.

Want to make your own? The mod format is documented in MODDING.md.
